Our Origin Story

The three founders were good friends in college, all passionate about video games. After college graduation, each of them worked for a business that did not bring them much excitement. One friend was great in IT, one was great in research, and the other was a great digital artist. Every weekend, they would play games online with each other to make up for their uneventful career lives.

As time went on, they all realized that they had the skills to turn their passion into a career. After some careful planning, Gridlock Interactive was born! Today, we operate as a mid-size video game company dedicated to creating unforgettable console and PC experiences, expanding worlds through DLC, and supporting active multiplayer communities across the globe.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What platforms do Gridlock Interactive games launch on?

We build games primarily for PC (Steam/Epic Games Store) as well as PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S consoles.

How can I participate in upcoming closed beta playtests?

Our UX research team led by Rodney regularly invites community members to test early builds! Follow our social channels or sign up for our newsletter to get notified when test applications open.

Are DLC packages included with the base game purchase?

Major story expansions and cosmetic DLCs are sold separately, but all core balance updates, server fixes, and seasonal events are delivered free to all owners of the game.
